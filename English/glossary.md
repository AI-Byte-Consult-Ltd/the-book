# Translation glossary

Style rules: professional literary English, no em dashes (use hyphens), straight quotation marks for dialogue, section breaks as `* * *`.

- Верховный - the High Priest
- Первые - the First Ones
- Эпос - the Epic
- начальник войска - the commander of the army
- Дом Жизни - the House of Life
- барыня - Madam / the mistress (butler's speech)
- сударь - sir
- Поручик - Lieutenant
- Порта - the Porte
- Дворянское гнездо - A Nest of Gentlefolk
- Появление - The Appearance

- Чати - the Vizier
- номарх / ном - nomarch / nome
- Древнейший Эпос - the Most Ancient Epic
- Библиотека - the Library
- Империя - the Empire

- Явь - Reality (section title; can be changed to "Waking Life" if preferred)
- До срока - Until the appointed time
- НИКС - NICS (Neural Intelligent Cognitive System)
